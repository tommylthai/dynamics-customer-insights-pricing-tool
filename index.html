Quick start: how to use this tool Choose a license, enter usage, and review the automatically updated estimate.
1

Choose the license

Use Customer Insights unless the customer owns at least 10 qualifying licenses for one eligible Dynamics 365 application. Only then select and confirm Customer Insights Attach.

2

Enter Journeys usage

Enter the number of entities contacted during the last 12 months. Then enter the highest monthly interaction volume, or complete the channel table if that total is unknown.

3

Enter Data usage

Enter the total number of unified customer profiles across all Customer Insights - Data environments in the tenant. Do not enter the number of raw source records.

4

Review the estimate

Use the result panel to see the selected capacity tiers, pack quantities, monthly estimate, and annual estimate. Validate the final amount with Microsoft or your licensing partner.

What information should I gather?

  • Whether the customer qualifies for Attach pricing.
  • Rolling 12-month interacted entities from Journeys quota usage.
  • Highest expected monthly email, SMS, push, voice, and custom-channel sends.
  • Total unified profiles across the tenant.

Simple example

Customer input: 75,000 interacted entities, 600,000 peak monthly interactions, and 300,000 unified profiles. Tool behavior: It compares 75,000 entities with 60,000 people required for interaction volume, uses 75,000, and automatically selects valid interacted and unified capacity tiers. Result: Review the monthly and annual list-price estimate in the right-hand panel.

Sources and assumptions

This is an estimator, not a quote. It excludes taxes, discounts, regional currency variations, SMS/telecom pass-through charges, and any separate Dynamics 365 app licenses.
  • Microsoft public pricing page for Dynamics 365 Customer Insights: microsoft.com Dynamics 365 Customer Insights pricing.
  • Tenant license assumptions: Customer Insights $1,700/month or Customer Insights Attach $1,000/month, with 10,000 interacted people and 100,000 unified people included. Attach pricing requires a qualifying Dynamics 365 application.
  • Official list prices are monthly amounts paid annually. Pricing was last verified against Microsoft sources on September 22, 2026.
  • Interacted people remain counted for 12 months after an interaction. The monthly interaction safe-use limit is 10 times the licensed interacted-people capacity.
  • Tier selection follows Microsoft pack thresholds and minimum quantities. At overlapping thresholds, the estimator selects the lowest-cost eligible tier.
