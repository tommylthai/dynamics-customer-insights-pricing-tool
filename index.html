What information should I gather?

  • Whether the customer qualifies for Attach pricing.
  • Total interactions expected in the busiest month.
  • Current interacted-people usage from Journeys quota limits, if available.
  • Total unified profiles across the tenant.

Simple example

Customer input: 600,000 interactions in the busiest month and 300,000 unified people. Tool behavior: It converts monthly interaction volume into required interacted capacity, then automatically selects valid interacted and unified capacity tiers. Result: Review the monthly and annual list-price estimate in the right-hand panel.

Sources and assumptions

This is an estimator, not a quote. It excludes taxes, discounts, regional currency variations, SMS/telecom pass-through charges, and any separate Dynamics 365 app licenses.
  • Microsoft public pricing page for Dynamics 365 Customer Insights: microsoft.com Dynamics 365 Customer Insights pricing.
  • Tenant license assumptions: Customer Insights $1,700/month or Customer Insights Attach $1,000/month, with 10,000 interacted people and 100,000 unified people included. Attach pricing requires a qualifying Dynamics 365 application.
  • Official list prices are monthly amounts paid annually. Pricing was last verified against Microsoft sources on September 22, 2026.
  • Interacted people remain counted for 12 months after an interaction. The monthly interaction safe-use limit is 10 times the licensed interacted-people capacity.
  • Tier selection follows Microsoft pack thresholds and minimum quantities. At overlapping thresholds, the estimator selects the lowest-cost eligible tier.
